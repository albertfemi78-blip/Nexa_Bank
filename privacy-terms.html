01 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NexaBank collects, uses, protects and manages information provided through our website and digital banking experience.

We are committed to treating personal information responsibly and maintaining appropriate safeguards to protect the information entrusted to us.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with NexaBank, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and basic identification details.
  • Contact information such as email address and telephone number.
  • Account and transaction information where applicable.
  • Information you submit through forms, applications or support requests.
  • Technical information such as browser, device and website usage information.
Important

Never share your password, PIN, OTP, card security code or other confidential banking credentials with anyone.

03 How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve our services, communicate with customers, process requests and maintain the security of our systems.

We may also use information to detect suspicious activity, prevent fraud, comply with applicable requirements and improve the functionality of our website.

04 Security

NexaBank takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Users are also responsible for protecting their login credentials and devices used to access banking services.

05 Cookies & Website Technologies

NexaBank may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences and understand how visitors interact with the website.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

06 Terms & Conditions

These Terms & Conditions describe the general rules governing your use of the NexaBank website and its available digital services.

By accessing or using the website, you agree to comply with these terms and all applicable laws and regulations.

07 Account Responsibilities

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and for taking reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access to their accounts.

  • Keep passwords and authentication information confidential.
  • Use strong and unique passwords where applicable.
  • Notify the bank promptly if suspicious activity is detected.
  • Do not allow unauthorized individuals to access your banking session.

08 Transactions & Services

Banking services may be subject to applicable limits, verification procedures, processing times, fees and other conditions.

Transaction availability may also depend on system maintenance, network availability, security checks or circumstances beyond reasonable control.

09 Acceptable Use

You agree not to misuse the NexaBank website or attempt to interfere with its security, functionality or availability.

  • Do not attempt unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
  • Do not introduce malicious software or harmful code.
  • Do not use the website for fraudulent or unlawful activities.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt or overload our systems.

10 Changes to These Terms

NexaBank may update these Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages when necessary to reflect changes to our services, practices or applicable requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective or updated date.

11 Contact NexaBank

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, these Terms & Conditions or the handling of your information, please contact NexaBank through our official support channels.
